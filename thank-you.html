Quick start

Four steps to a working funnel

  1. Open the Studio, pick your niche and see the funnel working. It loads example services, example questions and example prices for that trade. Every example number is labeled as an example.
  2. Put your real prices in, or let your AI do it. A low and a high per service. The "Let my AI fill this in" button copies a prompt you paste into Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or anything else, and you paste the answer back.
  3. Choose where leads go and send yourself a test lead. Your CRM, your email inbox with no CRM needed, or nowhere yet. The Studio asks whether the test lead arrived before you go live.
  4. Publish. The Publish tab walks you through putting the page live, and a HighLevel install guide is included.
