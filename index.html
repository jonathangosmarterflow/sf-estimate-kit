Does it work with my AI?

Yes. The builder prompt was tested on Claude, GPT and Gemini models, and the Config Doctor checks whatever comes back before you publish. The math is built in, so the AI can never invent a price or break the numbers. No AI? The Studio walks you through it by hand and you end up at the same funnel.

Do I need a Mac?

No. Any computer with a modern browser works: Mac, Windows or a Chromebook, with nothing to install. If you download the zip on Windows, right click it and choose Extract All first.

Where do my leads go?

You choose on the Publish tab: your CRM or automation tool (HighLevel, Zapier, Make or any webhook), your email with no CRM, or nowhere yet, so the page shows the price and your booking button only. The email option runs through a free third-party form-to-email service, so each lead's details pass through that service on the way to you. Either way, you send a test lead first and the Studio asks whether it arrived.

Does it give the customer a fixed price?

No, and that is on purpose. It gives a range, with a timeline and a short note on what drives the number. A fixed price sight unseen is a promise you have to break later. A range that is honest about what it does not know is the thing that survives the phone call.

Do I need HighLevel?

No. It is a single web page that works on any website. Host it anywhere, paste it into the site you already have, or open it straight off your desktop. A HighLevel install guide is included, because a lot of people reading this run on HighLevel.
