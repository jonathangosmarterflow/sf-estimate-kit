Quick start

Four steps to a working funnel

  1. Pick your niche and see the funnel working. It loads example services, questions and prices for that trade. Every example number is labeled, and Publish stays locked until you have confirmed or replaced them.
  2. Put your real prices in, or let your AI do it. A low and a high per service. The "Let my AI fill this in" button copies a prompt for Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or anything else, and the Config Doctor checks the answer you paste back.
  3. Choose where leads go and send yourself a test lead. Your CRM or automation tool, your email inbox with no CRM, or nowhere yet (the page shows the price and your booking button only). The Studio then asks whether the test lead arrived, so you never go live with leads going nowhere.
  4. Publish. Download one file with your settings baked in and put it on your site, or any host.Click "Get my live link" for a working public link and a QR code in seconds, with no hosting (it carries a small "Built with the Estimate Funnel Kit" footer). Or download one file with your settings baked in and host it yourself.
Three ways in

Pick the path that matches your setup

Path A

Claude Code does it

Unzip the kit, open the folder in Claude Code and say "build my estimate funnel". The skill ships inside the folder, so there is nothing to install. It interviews you, reads what you give it, writes the config and builds the funnel file. For anyone already working in Claude Code.

Path B

Any chatbot

Open the Studio, hit "Let my AI fill this in", paste the prompt into Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or anything else, and paste the answer back. The prompt was tested on Claude, GPT and Gemini models. The Config Doctor repairs the answer and shows you what changed. The kit does the math from your settings. Check the prices your AI entered before you publish. For everyone else.

Path C

No AI at all

Pick a preset, type your prices over the examples, drag the knobs, publish. Nothing about the kit requires a model. Often the fastest if you already know your numbers.

Installing it in HighLevel? Start with the quick install: about 5 minutes and zero custom fields. Each lead arrives as a contact with one note and one tag. The full field mapping is optional, for later. Both are in highlevel/INSTALL.md in the zip.
Building these for clients? AGENCY-PITCH.md in the zip covers how to offer an instant quote page to a local business: the email, the follow-up, a 60-second script and what to include in the offer.
